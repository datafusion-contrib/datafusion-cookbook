# ADBC

Connect to databases through
[ADBC (Arrow Database Connectivity)](https://arrow.apache.org/adbc/),
the Arrow-native equivalent of JDBC/ODBC: query results arrive as Arrow
record batches, avoiding row-to-column conversion.
